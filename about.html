About the Vertical Group System

Our History

The vertical group system at St. Peter's College was established to foster a greater sense of community and belonging among our students. By placing students in mixed-age house groups, we create a supportive environment where older students can mentor younger students, and where students of all ages can form lasting friendships.

Our Purpose

The purpose of the vertical group system is to provide a framework for pastoral care, competition, and leadership. The house system encourages healthy competition in a variety of academic, sporting, and cultural activities, while also providing a supportive network for students to turn to for guidance and support.

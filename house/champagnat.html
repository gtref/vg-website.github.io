Champagnat House

House Spirit

Champagnat House is known for its members' resilience, humility, and strong work ethic. Their house color is green, representing growth and vitality.

Points: 2nd with 380 points

Our History

Named after Saint Marcellin Champagnat, the founder of the Marist Brothers, Champagnat House embodies the Marist values of humility, simplicity, and modesty. The house is dedicated to fostering a supportive and inclusive environment where every student is encouraged to reach their full potential.

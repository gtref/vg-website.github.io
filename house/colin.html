Colin House

House Spirit

Colin House is characterized by its members' passion, courage, and creativity. Their house color is red, symbolizing strength and determination.

Points: 3rd with 350 points

Our History

Named after Jean-Claude Colin, the founder of the Society of Mary (Marist Fathers), Colin House is inspired by his vision of a compassionate and forward-thinking community. The house is committed to fostering a spirit of innovation and encouraging students to use their talents to make a positive impact on the world.

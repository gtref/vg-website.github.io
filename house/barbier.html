Barbier House

House Spirit

Known for their unwavering determination and strong sense of community, Barbier House is a force to be reckoned with. Their house color is a vibrant blue, symbolizing loyalty and wisdom.

Points: 1st with 420 points

Our History

Barbier House is named in honor of Sister Rosalie Barbier, a pioneer in Catholic education who was renowned for her dedication to fostering both academic excellence and compassionate service. The house strives to uphold her legacy by encouraging students to be leaders in both the classroom and the community.

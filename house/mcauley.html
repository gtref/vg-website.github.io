McAuley House

House Spirit

McAuley House is defined by its members' compassion, integrity, and commitment to service. Their house color is gold, representing excellence and generosity.

Points: 4th with 320 points

Our History

Named in honor of Catherine McAuley, the founder of the Sisters of Mercy, McAuley House is dedicated to upholding her legacy of compassionate service and social justice. The house fosters a strong sense of community and encourages students to use their talents to serve others.

McAuley House

House Spirit

McAuley House is known for its members' compassion, integrity, and commitment to service. Their house color is yellow, representing optimism and friendship.

Points: 4th with 320 points

Our History

Named after Catherine McAuley, the founder of the Sisters of Mercy, McAuley House is dedicated to upholding her legacy of compassionate service to others. The house is committed to fostering a culture of kindness and social responsibility, encouraging students to make a positive difference in their communities.

Welcome to the St. Peter's College Vertical Group System

Fostering community, competition, and spirit.

Our Houses

The heart of our vertical group system is our four houses: Barbier, Champagnat, Colin, and McAuley. Each house is a vibrant community where students build friendships, compete in a variety of events, and grow as individuals.

House Events

Throughout the year, students compete in a wide range of house events, including Swimming, St. Peter's Day, House Singing, and the Haka Competition. These events are a great way to show house spirit and have fun.

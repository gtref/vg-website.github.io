Swimming Competition

Event Details

The annual swimming competition is a highlight of the school year, bringing together students from all houses for a day of friendly competition and house spirit. The event features a wide range of races, from competitive sprints to fun relays, ensuring that everyone has a chance to participate and contribute to their house's success. It's a fantastic opportunity for students to showcase their swimming talents, cheer on their housemates, and build camaraderie.

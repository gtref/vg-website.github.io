House Singing

Event Details

The House Singing competition is a vibrant and energetic event that showcases the musical talents of our students. Each house comes together to perform a set song, rehearsing for weeks to perfect their harmonies and choreography. The event is a true testament to the creativity and teamwork of our students, and it's a fantastic opportunity for them to express themselves and show their house pride.

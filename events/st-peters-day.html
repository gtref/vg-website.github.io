St. Peter's Day

Event Details

St. Peter's Day is a special day of celebration and friendly competition. The day is filled with a variety of tabloid events, where houses compete against each other in a series of fun and challenging activities. It's a day of great energy and camaraderie, and a highlight of the school calendar.

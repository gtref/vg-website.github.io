St Peter's Day

Event Details

St. Peter's Day is a special day of celebration and service, where students come together to honor the school's patron saint. The day is filled with a variety of fun and engaging activities, from team-building challenges to community service projects. It's a time for students to reflect on the school's values, strengthen their bonds with their housemates, and give back to the community.

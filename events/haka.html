Haka Competition

Event Details

The Haka Competition is a powerful and moving event that celebrates the school's cultural heritage. Each house performs the school haka with passion and precision, showcasing their unity and strength. The competition is a deeply respected tradition that instills a sense of pride and belonging in our students, and it is a powerful expression of our school's identity.
